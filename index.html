🎬 HOW TO PLAY

CHAKRA-BHARAT — Game Architecture & Innovation

THE CENTRAL INNOVATION:
BUILD → SCAN → SYSTEM REMEMBERS → WORLD CHANGES → ADAPT → REBUILD
Physical infrastructure placed on the board dynamically changes how subsequent historical events (Droughts, Floods, Trade Booms) impact your civilization.
Four Core Resources:
🌾 Food: Nourishes population and agricultural strength.
💧 Water: Essential for life; conserved through stepwells to survive Droughts.
🪨 Materials: Timber & stone for constructing settlements.
🪙 Wealth: Minted gold for trading and expanding markets.
Four Pillars of Civilization:
• Farm: Increases Food production each turn.
• Water System (Baolis/Stepwells): Cuts Drought water loss from -3 down to -1! Protects against flood surges.
• Market (Bazaars & Shrenis): Doubles Wealth bounty during Trade Booms (+4 vs +2).
• Fort (Citadels/Durg): Defends stockpiles against disruptions and shortages.
Winning the Game (100 Points):
Evaluated across 6 criteria: Development (25), Resource Efficiency (20), Cultural Development (15), Sustainability (15), Adaptability (15), and Trade (10).
